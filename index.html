Consulting OS Dashboard

Consulting OS Dashboard is a private, single-user tool operated by Silicon Valley Insights Group LLC (SVIG). It runs on the owner's own computer and helps the owner manage his consulting schedule.

The app reads the owner's Google Calendar (read-only) to find open time slots for client calls and to show upcoming meetings on a private dashboard. It does not create, edit or delete calendar events, and it is not offered to any other user.
